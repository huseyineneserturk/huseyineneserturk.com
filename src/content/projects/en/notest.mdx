**Notest** is a study tool built during the first term of the Google AI &
Technology Academy bootcamp. It automatically generates quizzes from your notes,
helping students study more effectively through active recall.

- Automatic quiz generation from notes
- AI-assisted question creation
- An active-recall-focused study flow
