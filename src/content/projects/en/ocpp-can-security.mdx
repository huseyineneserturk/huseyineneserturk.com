This work examines the security of electric-vehicle (EV) charging infrastructure
across the **OCPP** (Open Charge Point Protocol) and **CAN Bus** layers. The
research identified and analyzed 10 distinct threat vectors.

- Threat modeling of EV charging infrastructure
- OCPP protocol security analysis
- CAN Bus communication-layer review
- Identification and assessment of 10 threat vectors
