For a while I wanted a corner of my own: a place to showcase my projects and
occasionally etch down my thoughts. Instead of an ordinary portfolio, I wanted
something a little **weird but different** — a *codex* that brings the warmth of
medieval manuscripts together with the cold precision of technology.

## Why a "codex"?

In the Middle Ages, codices were volumes where knowledge was meticulously copied
by hand and adorned with gold leaf. Today I bring that same care to code. This
site is a digital continuation of that tradition: pixels instead of parchment,
glowing circuit traces instead of ink.

<Marginalia>
Drop caps, marginal notes and filigree borders — all inspired by real
manuscripts.
</Marginalia>

## What you'll find here

- **Works** — the projects I'm building, with a chronicler's care.
- **Chronicle** — technical essays, things I've learned, and the occasional
  scattered thought.
- **Contact** — for an idea, or just to say hello.
